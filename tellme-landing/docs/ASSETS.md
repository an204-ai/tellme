# Landing assets

- `public/brand/`: copies of the Tellme blue wordmark, white wordmark, and brand icon supplied in the main project. Preserve these files as the source of truth; use the white icon over the navy app tile and the blue wordmark on white.
- `public/reactions/`: copies of Tellme's existing Like, Love, Haha, Wow, and Sad illustrations from the app, converted to WebP for this static site.
- `public/moments/coffee.webp`, `lunch.webp`, and `after-work.webp`: newly generated editorial illustrations for the landing-page mock story. They are illustrative marketing assets, not user stories or testimonials. The original prompts are beside this document.

All assets are local, so the pages do not depend on third-party image CDNs at runtime.
